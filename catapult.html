Mechanical Catapult Design & Testing

Aim / Goal

Design and manufacture a fully functioning mechanical catapult using a complete CAD-to-testing engineering workflow.

What we did

The system was developed in SolidWorks as a complete mechanical assembly, including the frame, arm mechanism, supports, threaded components and connecting hardware. We then fabricated and assembled the physical system, carried out mechanical performance testing and refined the design based on observed behaviour.

Results

The completed catapult operated successfully during practical testing and achieved full marks for performance, contributing to a High Distinction for the project.
