3D Bioprinted Brain Model for CTE

Aim / Goal

Develop a GelMA-based 3D bioprinted brain construct to investigate how mechanical impact affects cell viability in a simplified experimental model relevant to chronic traumatic encephalopathy research.

What we did

We refined bioprinting parameters, prepared GelMA constructs containing HDF cells, cultured the constructs in medium for one week and used fluorescence imaging to assess cell viability. The printing process was optimised to a 30 °C printhead temperature, 10 °C bed temperature, 10 kPa pressure and 16 mm/s print speed.

Results

The initial viability assessment showed that some cells survived the one-week culture period, although many were non-viable. During the impact experiment, the observed live-cell count decreased from 10 before the drop test to 0 afterward, while dead cells increased from 7 to 21.
