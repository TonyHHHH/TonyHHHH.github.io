Bone Scaffold Design & Mechanical Testing

Aim / Goal

Design and evaluate a porous scaffold concept for distal femur defects following osteosarcoma resection, balancing structural performance with the porous geometry required for a tissue-engineering scaffold.

What we did

The scaffold was modelled in SolidWorks with 45.72% porosity and evaluated under simulated vertical compression. Physical scaffold samples were then 3D printed and tested using a Shimadzu AGS-X universal testing machine, allowing experimental performance to be compared with simulation and between materials.

Results

PA12 outperformed Vero during physical compression testing. The strongest PA12 sample reached 25.010 MPa, compared with 13.251 MPa for the strongest Vero sample. The final comparison reported a simulated compressive strength of 28.87 MPa versus 25.01 MPa experimentally, showing reasonable agreement while highlighting the effect of real-world manufacturing and testing conditions.
