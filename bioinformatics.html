Bioinformatics Case Study – Genomic Diagnosis of Leukaemia

Aim / Goal

Use integrated bioinformatics workflows to investigate genomic and regulatory abnormalities in an acute promyelocytic leukaemia case study.

What we did

The analysis combined genomic variants, RNA expression, transcription-factor binding and histone-modification data. The workflow included sequence alignment, variant calling and genomic interpretation using tools such as Galaxy, Bowtie2, Mutect2 and public genomic databases, alongside RNA-seq and ChIP-seq analysis.

Results

The analysis retained 289 SNPs and 78 INDELs after filtering and identified substantially greater transcription-factor activity in the leukaemia samples than in normal controls. The project demonstrated how genomic, transcriptomic and epigenetic information can be integrated to investigate disease-associated molecular changes.
